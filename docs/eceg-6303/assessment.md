# Assessment — ECEG-6303

| Component | Weight | Timing |
|---|:---:|---|
| Tests (3) | 20% | Weeks 4, 8, 14 |
| Project and labs | 30% | Project released week 10, due week 15 |
| Final examination | 50% | Week 18 |

## Tests (20%)

Three 45-minute in-class tests, weighted 5% / 7% / 8% (increasing weight reflects
increasing difficulty). Closed book; one handwritten A4 formula sheet allowed.

## Project and labs (30%)

- **Labs (15%).** Seven lab reports — one per major chapter topic — submitted
  as Jupyter notebooks. Graded on correctness, interpretation, and code clarity.
- **Project (15%).** A self-contained implementation of one algorithm family
  (LMS, RLS, or spectrum estimation) applied to a real dataset. Report + code.
  **Attendance at all labs is mandatory** — missing more than 1 lab without
  documented cause results in loss of the lab mark.

## Final examination (50%)

3 h, closed book. Covers Chapters 1–5 in proportion to teaching hours
(~20% per chapter). Formula sheet allowed (one A4, handwritten). Non-programmable
calculator permitted.

## Grade boundaries

Standard MSc programme boundaries apply (A: 90+, B: 75–89, C: 60–74, F: <60).

## Course policy

- Attendance: **80% lecture, 100% lab** — mandatory.
- Late lab reports: 10% per calendar day, up to 3 days; after that, zero.
- Academic integrity: plagiarism and unauthorised AI use result in a zero on the
  affected component and a report to the Dean's office.
