# Shared References

Common DSP textbook shelf for both ECEG-6303 and ECEG-6423.

---

## Fundamentals (both courses)

- **Oppenheim & Schafer**, *Discrete-Time Signal Processing*, 3rd ed., Pearson, 2009 — the definitive reference on 1-D DSP.
- **Proakis & Manolakis**, *Digital Signal Processing: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications*, 4th ed., Pearson, 2006 — standard undergraduate text; good for background.
- **Mitra**, *Digital Signal Processing: A Computer-Based Approach*, 4th ed., McGraw-Hill, 2010 — well-illustrated; MATLAB examples.
- **Haykin & Van Veen**, *Signals and Systems*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2002 — for review of continuous-time concepts.

## Stochastic signals (ECEG-6303)

- **Papoulis & Pillai**, *Probability, Random Variables, and Stochastic Processes*, 4th ed., McGraw-Hill, 2002 — the standard reference.
- **Hayes**, *Statistical Digital Signal Processing and Modeling*, Wiley, 1996 — matches ECEG-6303 chapters 1–3.
- **Kay**, *Modern Spectral Estimation*, Prentice-Hall, 1988 — the spectrum-estimation classic.
- **Stoica & Moses**, *Spectral Analysis of Signals*, Prentice-Hall, 2005 — modern, comprehensive.

## Adaptive filtering (ECEG-6303)

- **Haykin**, *Adaptive Filter Theory*, 5th ed., Pearson, 2014 — the standard reference.
- **Sayed**, *Fundamentals of Adaptive Filtering*, Wiley, 2003 — modern, unified.
- **Diniz**, *Adaptive Filtering: Algorithms and Practical Implementation*, 5th ed., Springer, 2020 — implementation-focused.

## Multirate and wavelets (ECEG-6303)

- **Crochiere & Rabiner**, *Multirate Digital Signal Processing*, Prentice-Hall, 1983 — the classic.
- **Vaidyanathan**, *Multirate Systems and Filter Banks*, Prentice-Hall, 1993 — theoretical.
- **Mallat**, *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing*, 3rd ed., Academic Press, 2008 — comprehensive wavelets.

## Image processing (ECEG-6423)

- **Jain**, *Fundamentals of Digital Image Processing*, PHI, 1995 — matches the syllabus.
- **Gonzalez & Woods**, *Digital Image Processing*, 4th ed., Pearson, 2018 — the standard modern text.
- **Pratt**, *Digital Image Processing*, 4th ed., Wiley, 2007 — encyclopedic.
- **Szeliski**, *Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications*, 2nd ed., Springer, 2022 — free online.

## Speech processing (ECEG-6423)

- **Rabiner & Schafer**, *Digital Processing of Speech Signals*, Prentice Hall, 1978 — the classical reference.
- **Rabiner & Juang**, *Fundamentals of Speech Recognition*, Prentice Hall, 1993 — HMM reference.
- **Quatieri**, *Discrete-Time Speech Signal Processing*, Prentice Hall, 2001 — homomorphic methods.
- **Huang, Acero & Hon**, *Spoken Language Processing*, Prentice Hall, 2001 — comprehensive modern.

## Video processing (ECEG-6423)

- **Tekalp**, *Digital Video Processing*, 2nd ed., Prentice Hall, 2015 — the standard.
- **Wang, Ostermann & Zhang**, *Video Processing and Communications*, Prentice Hall, 2002 — matches the syllabus.
- **Richardson**, *The H.264 Advanced Video Compression Standard*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2010 — practical codec reference.
- **Sze, Budagavi & Sullivan (eds.)**, *HEVC: Algorithms and Architectures*, Springer, 2014.
