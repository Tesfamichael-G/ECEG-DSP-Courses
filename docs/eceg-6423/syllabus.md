## Objectives

On successful completion, students will be able to:

- Digitize images, speech, and video signals.
- Carry out image transforms.
- Restore images.
- Compress and reconstruct images.
- Process and apply speech signals using different analysis techniques and algorithms.
- Process and apply video signals using various analysis techniques.

## Competences

The student will understand and apply how images are processed and
reconstructed, how speech signals are analyzed and processed, and how video
signals are analyzed and processed.

## Chapter summaries

**Ch 1 — Continuous and Discrete Images and Systems.** Light, luminance, brightness,
contrast; the eye and monochrome vision model; image processing problems and
applications; the vision camera and digital processing system; 2-D sampling
theory and aliasing; image quantization (Lloyd–Max, dither); color images;
linear systems and shift invariance; Fourier transform, Z-transform; matrix
theory results; block matrices and Kronecker products.

**Ch 2 — Image Processing Techniques.** Image transforms (2-D orthogonal and
unitary, DFT, DCT, DST, Walsh–Hadamard, Haar, Slant, KL, SVD). Image enhancement
(point ops, histogram, spatial filtering, edge enhancement, colour enhancement).
Image restoration (observation models, sources of degradation, inverse and
Wiener, geometric mean filter, nonlinear filters, smoothing splines, constrained
least squares). Image compression and reconstruction from projections (image
data rates, pixel coding, predictive and transform coding, BTC, wavelet coding,
colour image coding, Radon transform, back-projection, algebraic restoration).

**Ch 3 — Introduction to Digital Speech Processing.** Digital speech processing,
transmission and storage, synthesis, speaker verification, speech recognition,
production mechanism, classification of sounds, models of production.

**Ch 4 — Time and Frequency Domain Speech Processing.** Short-time analysis;
time-domain parameters (energy, magnitude, ZCR, ACF, pitch by parallel processing);
frequency-domain (STFT, filter bank, spectrogram, formant extraction, pitch
extraction, analysis–synthesis); homomorphic analysis and LPC of speech.

**Ch 5 — Applications of Speech Processing.** Speech recognition — specification
of a typical application, voice response systems, spectral estimation, dynamic
time warping, HMM. Speech synthesis — text-to-speech, VoIP. Speaker verification
and identification.

**Ch 6 — Digital Video Processing.** Video representation and modelling; video
sampling and rate conversion; 2-D and 3-D motion estimation; video coding;
compression standards; error control; streaming video.
