## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. **Digitize** images, speech, and video — model the human visual and auditory
   systems, apply sampling and quantization, understand colour spaces.
2. **Carry out image transforms** — DFT, DCT, DST, Walsh–Hadamard, Haar, Slant,
   Karhunen–Loève, and SVD — and choose between them for compression,
   enhancement, and analysis.
3. **Enhance and restore images** — point operations, histogram equalization,
   spatial filtering, edge enhancement, inverse and Wiener restoration,
   constrained least-squares.
4. **Compress images** — predictive coding, transform coding, vector quantization,
   block truncation, wavelet coding, colour coding; understand JPEG and JPEG 2000.
5. **Process speech** — time-domain and frequency-domain short-time analysis,
   pitch estimation, formant extraction, cepstral analysis, LPC.
6. **Recognize and synthesize speech** — dynamic time warping, hidden Markov
   models, text-to-speech, speaker verification.
7. **Process video** — sampling and rate conversion, 2D and 3D motion estimation,
   video coding, compression standards (H.264, HEVC), error control, streaming.
