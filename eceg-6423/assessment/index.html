    Assessment — ECEG-6423¶

    Component Weight Timing
    Tests (3) 20% Weeks 3, 6, 13
    Project + labs + assignments 30% Project out week 10, due week 16
    Final examination 50% Week 18

    Tests (20%)¶

    Three 45-min in-class tests, weighted 5% / 7% / 8%. Closed book; one handwritten A4 formula sheet.

    Project, labs, assignments (30%)¶

    • Labs (15%). Seven lab reports (Python or MATLAB), submitted as Jupyter notebooks. Graded on correctness, interpretation, code clarity.
    • Project (10%). Self-contained multimedia application (image denoising, speech recognition on a small vocabulary, or video motion analysis). Report
    • code, presented in week 16.
    • Assignments (5%). Written problem sets.
    • Attendance at all labs is mandatory — missing more than one without documented cause loses the lab mark.

    Final examination (50%)¶

    3 h, closed book. Covers Chapters 1–6 in proportion to teaching hours. Handwritten A4 formula sheet allowed. Non-programmable calculator permitted.

    Grade boundaries¶

    Standard MSc boundaries (A: 90+, B: 75–89, C: 60–74, F: <60).

    Policies¶

    • 80% lecture, 100% lab attendance — mandatory.
    • Late assignments: 10% per day, zero after 3 days.
    • Academic integrity: plagiarism and unauthorised AI use result in a zero on the affected component and a report to the Dean's office.
